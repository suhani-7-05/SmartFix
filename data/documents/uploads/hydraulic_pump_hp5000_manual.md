# Hydraulic Pump HP-5000 Technical Maintenance Manual
Equipment Model: HP-5000 (Equipment ID Series EQ-1000 to EQ-1999)

## 1. Overview
The HP-5000 is a high-pressure variable displacement axial piston pump designed for industrial hydraulic circuits. Standard operating pressure: 210 bar. Maximum peak pressure: 250 bar. Fluid recommendation: ISO VG 46 anti-wear hydraulic oil.

## 2. Safety Guidelines & Lockout/Tagout Procedures
WARNING: High pressure hydraulic fluid can penetrate human skin causing severe injury.
1. ALWAYS perform electrical Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) on the main drive motor before opening pump housing.
2. Depressurize the system completely using the manual bleed valve. Verify pressure gauge reads 0 bar before loosening any fitting.
3. Wear safety goggles, heat-resistant gloves, and protective footwear when inspecting thermal components.
4. DO NOT adjust pressure relief valves while system is under load.

## 3. Diagnostic Procedures for Low Hydraulic Pressure
Symptom: System pressure drops below 150 bar under standard load.

Diagnostic Steps:
1. Reservoir Level Inspection: Check fluid sight glass. If level is below MIN line, refill ISO VG 46 hydraulic fluid.
2. Inlet Filter Clogging: Inspect 10-micron inlet suction filter element (Part #HP-FLTR-05). Clogged filters cause cavitation and rapid pressure drop. Clean or replace element.
3. Pressure Relief Valve Calibration: Check setpoint on primary relief valve (Part #HP-VALV-210). Adjust setpoint screw until gauge reads 210 bar at nominal RPM.
4. Shaft Seal & Piston Ring Inspection: Inspect pump body for external oil leaks. Damaged Viton shaft seals (Part #HP-SEAL-01) reduce volumetric efficiency.

## 4. Maintenance History & Known Failure Modes
- Cavitation noise: Caused by aeration or restricted suction line.
- Overheating (>75°C): Caused by internal bypass leakage or low fluid level.
- Rapid pressure loss: Caused by relief valve spring fatigue or worn piston shoes.
