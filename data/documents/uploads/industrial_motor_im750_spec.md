# High-Torque Electric Motor IM-750 Technical Manual
Equipment Model: IM-750 (Equipment ID Series EQ-3000 to EQ-3999)

## 1. Overview
The IM-750 is a 75 kW 400V 3-phase AC induction motor used for industrial heavy machinery drives. Rated current: 135A. Full load speed: 1475 RPM. Enclosure rating: IP55. Insulation class: Class H.

## 2. Safety Rules & High Voltage Isolation
DANGER: High Voltage 400V 3-Phase Circuit. Risk of fatal electric shock.
1. Isolate main feeder breaker and verify Zero Energy State using calibrated multimeter before opening terminal box (Part #IM-TERM-01).
2. Discharge power factor correction capacitors before touching motor leads.
3. Ensure motor frame grounding conductor is intact and resistance to earth is below 1 Ohm.

## 3. Troubleshooting Overheating & Excessive Vibration
Symptom: Stator winding temperature exceeds 120°C or vibration exceeds 4.5 mm/s RMS.

Diagnostic Steps:
1. Ventilation Fan & Shroud: Inspect rear cooling fan (Part #IM-FAN-750) and housing fins for dust accumulation or broken blades.
2. Current Unbalance Test: Measure phase currents (L1, L2, L3) using clamp ammeter. Unbalance > 5% indicates winding turn-to-turn fault or supply voltage imbalance.
3. Bearing Degradation: Use vibration analyzer to check drive-end (DE) bearing (Part #IM-BRG-6314) and non-drive-end (NDE) bearing (Part #IM-BRG-6312). High envelope spectrum peak indicates bearing race pitting.

## 4. Recommended Maintenance Schedule
- Monthly: Measure insulation resistance (Megger test @ 1000V DC). Minimum acceptable resistance: 100 Mohm.
- Quarterly: Re-grease bearings with Polyurea synthetic grease (15 grams per bearing).
- Annually: Perform dynamic balancing of motor rotor assembly.
