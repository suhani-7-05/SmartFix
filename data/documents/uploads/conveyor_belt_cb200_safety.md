# Industrial Conveyor Belt CB-200 Operations & Safety Guide
Equipment Model: CB-200 (Equipment ID Series EQ-2000 to EQ-2999)

## 1. Overview
The CB-200 is a heavy-duty industrial belt conveyor system designed for automated package and material transport. Maximum speed: 2.5 m/s. Motor drive: 15 kW 3-phase induction motor with gearbox ratio 1:30.

## 2. Safety Precautions & Hazardous Conditions
BLOCKED OPERATION WARNING: Inspecting moving conveyor belts without stopping the drive motor is strictly prohibited.
1. NEVER place hands, tools, or clothing near head/tail pulley pinch points while motor is energized.
2. Emergency Stop Pull-Cord: Test pull-cord switches along both sides of conveyor weekly. Pulling cord must immediately trip main circuit breaker.
3. Belt Jamming: If belt jams, press E-STOP, apply padlocks to main isolator switch, and verify drive motor is dead before clearing jammed material.

## 3. Diagnostic Procedures for Belt Overheating & Slippage
Symptom: Belt drive drum slips or emits burning rubber smell.

Diagnostic Steps:
1. Tension Alignment: Measure belt deflection at center span. Adjust tail pulley tension bolts (Part #CB-BOLT-M20) evenly until tension matches 45 kN specification.
2. Drive Drum Lagging Wear: Inspect rubber lagging on drive drum (Part #CB-DRUM-200). Worn lagging reduces friction coefficient, causing belt slippage.
3. Roller Bearing Lubrication: Check roller bearings for overheating. Grease sealed bearings using lithium-complex EP2 grease every 500 operating hours.
